Privacy Policy

Qur'anic Tafsir Research Canvas+ (QTRC+)

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. What QTRC+ collects

When you sign in with Google, QTRC+ may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile image. QTRC+ uses this information to identify your account and provide account-based features.

2. Research data

Research canvases and related content that you save in QTRC+ may be stored in our application database and associated with your user account. We use this data to provide your saved canvases and account features.

3. AI processing

When you use QTRC+ analysis or brainstorming features, the content you submit may be sent to third-party AI infrastructure used to provide those features. QTRC+ does not place private API keys in the browser.

4. Third-party services

QTRC+ uses third-party services for authentication, database storage, hosting, and AI processing. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functions.

5. Security

QTRC+ uses account-based access controls for saved user data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Your choices

You may stop using QTRC+ at any time. You may also contact us regarding account or personal-data questions.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the QTRC+ project owner at the developer contact email associated with the application.
