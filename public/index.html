QUR’ANIC TAFSIR RESEARCH CANVAS+

Build the research
before you build the canvas.

QTRC+ is an epistemic research workspace for Qur’anic tafsir. Develop an idea, test its coherence, and gradually turn it into a defensible research architecture.

WHAT QTRC DOES

Think. Test. Structure.

Bring an idea, a question, or your own writing. QTRC maps what is established, surfaces what remains open, and keeps the research architecture visible.

THE WORKSPACE

11 connected blocks

Topic, Research Question, Objective, Corpus, Context, Sources, Theory, Method, Analysis, Contribution & Novelty, and Title work as one system.

YOUR CONTROL

You choose the epistemic stance.

Choose a mode and a level. That selection determines how QTRC behaves—from exploratory development to strict validation and expert-level scrutiny.

HOW QTRC WORKS

One research space. Two modes. Three depths.

Your selection is the working contract for the session.

RESEARCH GUIDE

Use QTRC as a thinking space,
not a checklist.

QTRC helps you structure, assess, and develop Qur’anic tafsir research while keeping scholarly judgment with the researcher.

Start with the problem.

A topic is a starting point, not yet a research problem. Use the research session to clarify what you actually want to understand, explain, compare, or construct.

Keep the 11 blocks connected.

Topic, Research Question, Objective, Corpus Qur’aniyyah, Context, Data & Sources, Theoretical Framework, Method of Tafsir, Analysis Strategy, Contribution & Novelty, and Title are interdependent. You do not have to complete them in a rigid order.

Thinking ModeExplore possibilities and develop provisional structures.

Core principle

QTRC is a methodological assistant. It should not silently invent a corpus, theory, method, source, quotation, or scholarly conclusion that the researcher has not established.

ABOUT QTRC+

Research thinking for
Qur’anic tafsir.

QTRC+ is an epistemic research workspace designed to help researchers structure, examine, and develop Qur’anic tafsir research designs.

QTRC makes relationships between research components visible. It helps a researcher move from an initial intuition toward a coherent research architecture without taking the scholarly decision away from the researcher.

The workspace is built around an 11-block research canvas and a supervisor-style research session. Your selected working mode and research depth determine how QTRC approaches the research.

Structure Make research dependencies visible.
Assess Surface epistemic and methodological gaps.
Orchestrate Keep the research architecture coherent as it develops.
