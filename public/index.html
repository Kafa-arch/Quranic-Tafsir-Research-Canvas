The QTRC architecture

QTRC is a conceptual research-thinking tool, not a rigid checklist. Its 11 blocks are connected and can be explored non-linearly while remaining structurally coherent.

Research depth

Basic

Descriptive & exploratory. The theory mainly supports description and the research generally works with a clear, systematic application of a method.

Intermediate

Analytical & comparative. The theory becomes a critical lens and the research can evaluate, compare, or synthesize interpretations.

Expert

Constructive & epistemological. The research aims toward new conceptual, theoretical, or paradigmatic construction.

Proposed Canvas

Research Guide

QTRC+ is a methodological workspace, not a replacement for scholarly judgment.

Structure

The recommended learning order is sequential, but the canvas itself remains flexible and non-linear. A researcher can begin from another block when the research logic calls for it.

Assess

Check whether the corpus, context, sources, theory, tafsir method, and analysis strategy actually support the research question.

Progress

Move from descriptive work at Basic, to analytical and comparative work at Intermediate, then toward constructive work at Expert.

Scope

QTRC is designed for interpretative Qur’anic tafsir research, especially thematic and comparative studies, with limited conditional use for tafsir-figure studies that analyze meaning construction. It is not designed as the primary framework for empirical Living Qur’an studies or purely technical ‘Ulūm al-Qur’ān research.

Proses pengisian dan evaluasi kanvas dilakukan melalui antarmuka digital berbasis AI (QTRC+), tanpa menjadikan sistem AI sebagai sumber data, penafsir, atau pengambil keputusan ilmiah.”

Qur’anic Tafsir Research Canvas+

QTRC+ is an epistemic orchestration system designed to help researchers structure, assess, and develop Qur’anic tafsir research designs.

What QTRC+ does

It makes relationships between research components visible, identifies epistemic and methodological gaps, and helps researchers move from exploratory ideas toward a coherent research architecture.
